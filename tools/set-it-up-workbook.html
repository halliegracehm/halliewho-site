Hi, I'm Hallie.

I spent years trying to change everything at once and wondering why nothing stuck. What finally worked was smaller than I expected: one habit, made tiny, done on purpose, even on the hard days.

That's what this program is. You put things down with Set It Down. Here, you build something up.

How it works

Week 1Pick ItChoose one habit and make it tiny.
Week 2Stack ItAttach it to something you already do.
Week 3Protect ItPlan for the hard days and the missed ones.
Week 4Own ItLook back, level up, and decide what's next.

Each week

  1. Open my email and read the week's focus. It takes about five minutes.
  2. Fill in that week's worksheet in this workbook.
  3. Mark your tracker every day (it's at the back).
  4. Answer the check-in questions at the end of the week.
The only rule: never miss twice. Missing one day is life. Missing two days in a row is a new habit starting. If you slip, just get back on the next day.
